Kongming Chess

Kongming Chess (also known as Peg Solitaire, Solo Noble, or Marble Solitaire) is a one-player board puzzle where you leap a peg over a neighbor along straight lines into an empty hole and remove the jumped piece. The ambition is to clear the board until only a single peg remains, ideally ending back in the center.

It surfaced at the court of Louis XIV by 1697 through Mercure galant, which published the rules and problems for the cross-shaped board we still play, marking the earliest printed reference to the game.

The standard setup fills every hole except the center; each valid move removes one peg so the board gradually empties.
