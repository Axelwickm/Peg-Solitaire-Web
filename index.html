Peg Solitaire

What is Peg Solitaire (Kongming Chess)?

Peg Solitaire (also known as Kongming Chess, Solo Noble, Solo Goli, Marble Solitaire, or simply Solitaire) is a one-player board puzzle that asks you to jump a peg over a neighboring peg into an empty hole and remove the jumped piece, gradually clearing the board until just one peg remains, ideally back in the center.

Start with every allowed hole filled except the center, select a peg, and jump it over an adjacent peg into an empty hole to remove the jumped peg. Repeat until only one peg remains. This build includes helper hints and an auto-solver to guide your best moves.

The first known mention of the game appears in 1697 at the court of Louis XIV, when the French magazine Mercure galant described the cross-shaped board, the rules, and sample problems, making that edition the earliest printed reference to the puzzle.

The standard setup fills every allowed hole except the center so every move removes exactly one peg, and the challenge is to keep jumping along straight paths until the board is empty save for a single survivor.
